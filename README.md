# SiAgent

### Build Intelligence. Make It Real.

**An independent exploration of superintelligence and AI agents.**

[siagent.build](https://siagent.build)

---

Intelligence should do more than answer questions.

It should understand context, use tools, coordinate with other agents, learn from feedback, and turn intentions into outcomes.

**SiAgent is about exploring what's possible — and building toward it.**

Not just better prompts.<br/>
Not just another chatbot.<br/>
But a new way to make intelligence useful.

## What we believe

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Beyond Chat** | Intelligence should act, not just respond. |
| **Systems over Demos** | Real capability requires reliable systems, not impressive screenshots. |
| **Intelligence through Coordination** | Multiple agents should work together to solve complex problems. |
| **Memory and Context** | Intelligence should understand more than the current conversation. |
| **Build the Future** | Turn ideas into experiments, and experiments into reality. |

## Areas of exploration

**Agents** — Moving from conversational AI to autonomous action.

**Memory** — Building persistent context and learning from experience.

**Coordination** — Enabling agents to collaborate on complex tasks.

**Infrastructure** — Creating the foundations for reliable, scalable intelligence.

These are directions to explore, not limitations on what SiAgent may become.
